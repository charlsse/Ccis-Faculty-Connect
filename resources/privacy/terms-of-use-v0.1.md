> INTERNAL DRAFT - NOT EFFECTIVE TERMS. Version 0.1; prepared 9 October 2026. Operator authority and legal review are outstanding.

## Intended service

FacultyConnect supports existing faculty-club account, announcement, contribution and record workflows. Accounts are issued by authorized administrators. There is no public self-registration route. These proposed terms do not establish University of Makati sponsorship or an institutional processing mandate.

## Proposed user responsibilities

Use only an account assigned to you; protect your password, authenticator and recovery codes; provide accurate information; and report suspected account misuse through a contact that must be approved before publication. Do not share credentials, access another person's records without authority, upload malicious content, misrepresent transactions, or submit unrelated personal data in notes or proofs.

## Proposed role responsibilities

Administrators should grant only authorized access. Collection reviewers should base decisions on evidence and established club procedures, not solely on uploaded screenshots. Users should review displayed amounts and references and use the documented correction process. The actual code distinguishes submitted, pending, rejected and verified payment states; these terms do not create a refund or reversal service that the application does not implement.

## Content and privacy

Users should have appropriate authority to upload photographs, announcement content and information concerning other people. The final Privacy Notice must separately explain processing. Agreement to these terms does not constitute consent to every possible processing purpose, waive data-subject rights, or authorize unrelated secondary use.

## Account restrictions and disputes - proposed

Authorized administrators may use existing deactivation/session-revocation functions under an approved access policy. The grounds, accountable decision maker, notice, review/appeal path, record correction and dispute-handling procedure still need approval. Do not promise an existing support channel or a response time that has not been established.

## Availability and changes - requires review

Maintenance, supported service scope, contact details, governing arrangements and legally appropriate responsibility provisions remain to be decided. This draft supplies no blanket liability exclusion or claim of uninterrupted availability. Approved changes must receive a new version and effective date; previous approved versions must be retained as evidence of what a user accepted.

## Publication conditions

Before use, confirm the operator and authority; approve acceptable-use and dispute procedures; finish the Privacy Notice; verify the responsible contact; and approve the effective date. The acceptance checkbox remains absent while these conditions are unresolved. No public user is bound to this draft by using the application.
