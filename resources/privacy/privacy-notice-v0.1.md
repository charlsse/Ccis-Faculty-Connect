> INTERNAL DRAFT - NOT AN EFFECTIVE PRIVACY NOTICE. Version 0.1; prepared 9 October 2026. Requires approval before publication or acknowledgment.

## Purpose and scope

This proposed notice concerns FacultyConnect account administration, faculty profiles, club announcements, contributions, payment records, and account security. Its description of collection is based on the application audit. It is not an assertion of legal compliance or University of Makati endorsement.

## Information currently handled

Accounts contain names, faculty/employee ID, email, department, position, role and account status. Optional profile information includes contact number, biography and photograph. Authentication handles password hashes, two-factor secrets/recovery information, verification/reset state, IP/browser information, sessions and trusted-device records.

Contribution workflows handle collection assignments, amounts, dates, status, transaction references, payment proof files, faculty notes, reviewer notes, verification decisions, waiver reasons and receipt snapshots. Uploaded proofs and free-text fields can incidentally contain information about other people. Announcements and uploaded images may also identify people. Logs and notifications support security and service operation.

## Sources, purposes and legal bases

Information is supplied by account administrators, users, collection officers and system activity. The proposed purposes are account access, faculty coordination, records maintenance, contribution verification, receipts, fraud/error prevention and security investigation.

A qualified reviewer must identify the applicable legal basis for **each purpose and data category**, including any sensitive personal information that may be submitted. Do not assume that use of the portal, institutional affiliation, or a general checkbox establishes a valid basis. Purposes not supported by an approved basis must be removed, restricted, or supported by separately designed consent where appropriate.

## Access and recipients

Existing application permissions differentiate personal records, directory access, account administration, announcement management, payment oversight and payment management. Actual authorized users and organizational responsibilities require approval and periodic review. Server, database, storage, backup and mail operators may have access outside application roles; their identities, contracts, locations and safeguards remain to be confirmed.

Profile photographs and announcement/site images use public storage URLs. The privacy implications and necessity of this visibility need review. Payment proofs and receiving-account QR images use private storage and authorized application routes.

The interface requests fonts from Google domains. Configured SMTP transports receive invitation and account-related email data. PayMongo code is test-only and was disabled in the audited local configuration; the optional gateway table was absent. Manual payments can involve external payment applications chosen in the existing workflow. No cross-border-transfer assurance or provider approval is established by this draft.

## Retention, protection and disposal

No approved general retention schedule was found. Technical token expiry does not mean records, logs, files or backups have been erased. Proposed lifecycle controls are recorded separately for review; no deletion schedule is promised here.

The code implements selected encryption, password hashing, role/record authorization, CSRF protection, two-factor controls and private proof access. These are limited implementation findings, not a guarantee that all data is encrypted or that deployment operations are secure. Deployment TLS, backups, incident handling, operator access and restoration practices require verification.

## Requests and choices - process awaiting approval

The proposed process would let a person ask about their data, request correction or exercise applicable rights, with proportionate identity verification and review of lawful exceptions and record-preservation requirements. No unverified mailbox, response deadline, automatic erasure promise or functioning request workflow is represented as available. Account closure is not equivalent to erasure of financial records.

The [Data Privacy Act of 2012, Republic Act No. 10173](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act-/) is the statutory reference for review of purposes, rights and obligations. A designated reviewer must approve the operational request and complaint process, responsible contact and applicable timelines before this notice is published.
